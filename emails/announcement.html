We're Down to Split, an ND student-built project that helps you find other students to split a ride with to or from the airport. You can join an existing trip on our trip board, or you can post your own.

It's simple:

1. Post when you're taking a rideshare to the airport (or make a private trip and share the invite link!)
2. Wait for people to join. You'll get each other's contact info to coordinate.
3. Split the ride and the fare. A $140 trip comes to about $35 each with four people.

Try creating a trip! It's super easy, and you can check back anytime to see if anyone wants to join you.
