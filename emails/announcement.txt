We're Down to Split, an ND student-built project that helps you find others at ND to split a ride with to or from the airport. You can join an existing trip on our trip board, or you can post your own.

Example trip:

  Fri, Oct 16 @ 2:30 PM
  Main Circle -> O'Hare T1
  Trip total $140, about $35/person
  3 of 4 seats filled

It's simple:
1. Post when you're taking a rideshare to the airport (or make a private trip and share the invite link!)
2. Wait for people to join. You'll get each other's contact info to coordinate.
3. Split your rideshare and the fare. A $140 trip comes to about $35 each with four people.

Try creating a trip! It's super easy, and you can check back anytime to see if anyone wants to join you.

Create a trip: https://downtosplit.app
(Free. ND emails only. Sign in with your @nd.edu Google account.)

The more eyes on this, the more rides get split, so spread the word!

The Down to Split team
