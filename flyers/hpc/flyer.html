What is Down to Split?

A free trip board for ND students heading to the airport. Find students going to the airport at the same time as you and share one Uber or Lyft to O'Hare, Midway or South Bend.

How it works
  1. 1Find or post
    Browse trips leaving near your time, or post your own.
  2. 2Others join
    Students going your way join. You get each other's contact info.
  3. 3Ride and split
    Take one Uber or Lyft together and split the fare.
Plus, it's on your schedule. Your group picks the time. No waiting on a bus.
