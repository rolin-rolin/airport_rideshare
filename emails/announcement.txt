We're Down to Split, an ND student-built project. We got sick of inconvenient bus times to O'Hare and Midway, and went broke on upcharged Ubers to South Bend Airport, so we built a fix.

Post when you're taking a rideshare to the airport, and anyone going at the same time can join your trip. You split the cost, and getting to the airport feels a little better, timewise and moneywise.

Try creating a trip! It takes less than a minute, and you can check back anytime to see if anyone wants to join you and split the cost.

Create a trip: https://downtosplit.app
(Free. Sign in with your @nd.edu Google account.)

The more eyes on this, the more rides get split, so spread the word!
